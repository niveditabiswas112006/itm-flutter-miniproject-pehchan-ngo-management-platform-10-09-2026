# Pehchan NGO Management Platform

A comprehensive NGO platform where organizations create social events (tree plantation, plastic awareness, blood donation, etc.), people join as volunteers (event-specific or lifetime), and receive certificates upon event completion. The system manages money and material donations with automated wallet transactions for NGO expenses.

## Key Focus Areas
- Event creation & volunteer management
- Certificate generation system
- Wallet management (donations & expenses)
- Volunteer types (event-specific & lifetime)
- Money & material donation tracking
